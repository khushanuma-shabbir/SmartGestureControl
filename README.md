### 🔍 About the Project

**SmartGestureControl** is a **real-time hand gesture recognition system** that allows you to control your PC using only your **index finger**.  
It leverages **OpenCV**, **MediaPipe**, and **PyAutoGUI** to provide seamless interaction without a mouse or touch input.

With this tool, you can:  
- Swipe between applications  
- Scroll web pages up and down  
- Keep a camera feed always on top for gesture tracking  

---

### 📸 Features

- ✅ Detects index finger movement accurately  
- ✅ Swipe gestures:  
  ➡️ Switch between apps (Alt + Tab)  
  ⬆️⬇️ Scroll up and down using mouse wheel  
- ✅ Gesture feedback overlay on camera feed  
- ✅ Floating camera window (always on top)  
- ✅ Works with external or phone camera
